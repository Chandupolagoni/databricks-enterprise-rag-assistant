# SOP: Voluntary Corporate Actions

## Notification

The Corporate Actions team must notify affected clients of a voluntary corporate action, such as a tender offer or rights issue, within 1 business day of receiving the announcement.

## Client Elections

The internal election deadline is 2 business days before the market deadline. Clients who do not respond receive the default option stated in the announcement.

## Reconciliation

Entitlements are reconciled against the custodian on the pay date. Breaks must be resolved within 3 business days.
