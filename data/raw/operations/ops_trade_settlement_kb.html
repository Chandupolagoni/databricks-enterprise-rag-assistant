KB: Trade Settlement Failures

Overview

US equities settle on T+1. A failed settlement occurs when securities or cash are not delivered on the settlement date.

Common Causes

The most common causes are mismatched standing settlement instructions, insufficient securities in the client account and late trade affirmation.

Resolution

Contact the counterparty within 2 hours of the fail notification. If the fail persists for 3 days, escalate to the Settlements team lead. Fails older than 5 days must be reported to the Operational Risk team.

CSDR Penalties

For European trades, cash penalties under CSDR are applied daily and are charged back to the responsible desk.
