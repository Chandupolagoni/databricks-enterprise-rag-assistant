SOP: Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment
=======================================

Tiering
-------
Vendors are tiered as critical, high, moderate or low based on data access and service criticality.

Due Diligence
-------------
Critical and high vendors require a SOC 2 Type II report, a security questionnaire and a financial review before contract signature.

Reassessment
------------
Critical vendors are reassessed annually; low-tier vendors every 3 years.
