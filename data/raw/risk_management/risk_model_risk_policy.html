Model Risk Management Policy

Scope

Applies to all quantitative models, including machine learning and generative AI models. The policy is governed by Federal Reserve guidance SR 11-7.

Validation

High-tier models must be independently validated before production use and revalidated every 12 months. Medium-tier models are revalidated every 24 months.

Generative AI

Generative AI use cases must document hallucination controls, human oversight and a monitoring plan before approval by the Model Risk Committee.
