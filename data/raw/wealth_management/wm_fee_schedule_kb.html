KB: Advisory Fee Schedule

Tiered Fees

The annual advisory fee is 1.00 percent on the first 1 million USD, 0.75 percent on the next 4 million USD, and 0.50 percent on assets above 5 million USD.

Billing

Fees are billed quarterly in advance, based on the account value on the last business day of the prior quarter.

Waivers

Fee waivers above 25 percent require approval from the regional managing director.
