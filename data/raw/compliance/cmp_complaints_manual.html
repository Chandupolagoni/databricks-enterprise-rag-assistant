Client Complaints Handling Manual

Definition

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction from a client, written or verbal, about a product, service or employee of Meridian Trust & Co.

Acknowledgement

All complaints must be logged in the Complaints Register and acknowledged to the client within 2 business days.

Resolution Timeline

Standard complaints must be resolved within 15 business days. Complaints involving alleged fraud, discrimination or a regulatory breach are classified as escalated and must be routed to Compliance within 1 business day.

Root Cause

Each quarter the Complaints Committee reviews root causes and reports trends to the Operational Risk Committee.
