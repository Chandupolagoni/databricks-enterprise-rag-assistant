# Gifts and Entertainment Policy

## Limits

Employees may accept gifts with a value of up to 100 USD per giver per calendar year. Business entertainment up to 250 USD per person per event is allowed if the giver attends.

## Pre-approval

Any gift or entertainment above these limits requires pre-approval from the employee's manager and Compliance through the Gifts Register.

## Government Officials

Gifts of any value to government officials are prohibited without written approval from the Chief Compliance Officer.
