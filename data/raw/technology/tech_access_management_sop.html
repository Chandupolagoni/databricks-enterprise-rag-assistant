SOP: User Access Management

Provisioning

Access requests are submitted in the IAM portal and approved by the data owner. Privileged access additionally requires approval from the Information Security team.

Recertification

Managers recertify their team's access every quarter. Privileged access is recertified monthly.

Leavers

Access for leavers must be revoked within 24 hours of the termination date.
