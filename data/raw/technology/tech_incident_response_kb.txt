KB: Cybersecurity Incident Response
===================================

Severity
--------
Severity 1 incidents involve confirmed client data exposure or a ransomware event. Severity 1 incidents must be reported to the CISO within 1 hour.

Containment
-----------
Isolate affected hosts from the network, preserve logs and disable compromised accounts.

Regulatory Notice
-----------------
If client data is affected, Legal decides on regulatory notification. Banking regulators must be notified within 36 hours of determining a notification incident.
