# Generative AI Acceptable Use Policy

## Approved Tools

Only firm-approved generative AI tools, such as the Enterprise Knowledge Assistant, may be used with internal data.

## Prohibited Data

Client personal information, material non-public information and credentials must never be entered into external AI tools.

## Human Review

Outputs used in client communications must be reviewed by a human before sending. The policy is owned by the Technology department and governed by the Model Risk Management Policy.
