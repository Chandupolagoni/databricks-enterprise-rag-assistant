# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year.

## Mandatory Block Leave

Employees in trading, payments and treasury roles must take a mandatory block leave of 10 consecutive business days each year as a fraud control.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks.
