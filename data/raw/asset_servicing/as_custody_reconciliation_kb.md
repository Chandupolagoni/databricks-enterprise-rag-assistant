# KB: Custody Cash and Position Reconciliation

## Frequency

Cash and positions are reconciled daily against sub-custodians using the ReconOne platform.

## Ageing

Breaks older than 5 business days are reported to the Asset Servicing manager. Breaks older than 30 days are escalated to Operational Risk and require a written root cause.

## Ownership

Asset Servicing owns the reconciliation process; the Technology department owns the ReconOne platform.
